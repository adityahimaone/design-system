Architectural laws Gloaming · V1.0 · plate-source.json
  • One measured ground. Eleven strips off one painting become one gradient; nothing is stacked on it.
  • Glass never carries type. periwinkle on zenith is 2.49:1, so a 1px mullion and a solid oat plate do the reading.
  • One accent, on the action. Oat on apricot is 1.88:1; the button uses ink on apricot at 7.6:1.
  • The plate never changes. Night removes the warm band, not the paper. Primitives never flip.
