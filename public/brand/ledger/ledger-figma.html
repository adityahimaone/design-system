/* ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Ledger — Tactile Glass & Crisp Grid · Indigo Harbor
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Every value here is scoped to `[data-ledger]`. The site's own shadcn role
   tokens (`--background`, `--card`, `--border`…) stay untouched at :root; this
   file swaps them for the duration of one subtree, which is the same move the
   Sunnyside method calls a "night surface as a scope, never a second palette".

   Three layers, in order: primitive → semantic → the surface recipes.
   A theme flip only ever rewrites the semantic layer.

   The material is harbor water, not zinc: every neutral carries chroma toward
   hue 267 at the same lightness a zinc step would have had. That is what makes
   the paper read cool rather than grey, and it is why the indigo signal does
   not look pasted on. The deep pole (#001b3c) is the one colour that is not on
   the lightness ramp — it is the water itself, used for the mark plate and for
   every float shadow.
   ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── */

/* Night: next-themes puts `.dark` on <html>, so the scope has to answer to both
   an ancestor class and its own class (for a standalone render).
