Why does Doop show "Frames must be at most 10,000px"?
Figma has a strict internal limit: no frame can exceed 10,000px. When all 8 sheets are stacked vertically, the page totals ~13,500px. Choose one of the fixes below to import cleanly:

Option A: 2-Column Canvas Grid (< 10,000px)

Arranges all 8 sheets in a 2-column side-by-side artboard canvas (~2,960px × ~7,400px), well under Figma's 10,000px limit on both sides.
